Case study · Healthcare / B2B Support

New Support and Help solution for Laerdal Medical

Laerdal customers were resolving fewer issues on their own than they should have been, and defaulting to the help desk instead. This is the self-service solution built to close that gap, starting with a standalone version.

Role
UX design, research, interface design
Scope
Research, UX design
Format
Iterative, data-driven process
Status
Standalone version delivered
Link to Laerdal Support page

Problem

Two frustrations, one root cause

Customers struggled to resolve issues on their own, which pushed call volume onto a help desk that shouldn't have been the first stop. Two things were driving it: finding relevant content was hard, and the support material itself was thin: onboarding guidance, best-use-case clarity and product-selection help were all missing, made worse by how large the product range had grown.

The problem statement was simple: a lack of quick, independent issue resolution. Customers called the help desk because they couldn't quickly find their own answer.

Research

Understanding the ecosystem before designing the screens

The research spanned user interviews, a competitive analysis of support experiences elsewhere, ecosystem mapping across Laerdal's digital portals, a field trip to observe real end-user behaviour, and user personas and flows built from what came back. Defining KPIs helped shape strategy without becoming the only guide. Priorities shifted as the research did, and the process stayed flexible on purpose.

Approach

Four focus points

  • Clear navigation: an information architecture built for customers to find relevant resources quickly.
  • Comprehensive content: structured categories giving customers a full picture of their options for resolving an issue.
  • Troubleshooting possibility: a guided, step-by-step process for independent resolution, with clear pathways instead of a dead-end search box.
  • Efficient case communication: detailed information capture at case creation, including attachments and context, so internal specialists start with what they need instead of chasing it down.

Outcome

What the solution does

The redesigned Support page lets customers resolve issues independently or file a well-formed ticket through a deflective case-creation flow, rather than defaulting straight to a phone call. The project is still in progress: the previous solution is being replaced carefully, with user testing and feedback continuing to shape what gets built next.
